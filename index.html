About RecycleIT

RecycleIT is being developed to give businesses, schools and organisations a straightforward way to clear unwanted IT, electrical and office equipment.

Frequently asked questions

IT recycling and collection questions

Helpful information for businesses, schools and organisations planning an equipment clearance or regular collection.

Do you offer free IT recycling collections?

Free collections are available for qualifying quantities. Eligibility depends on the equipment type, quantity and collection location, so each request is reviewed before a collection is agreed.

What IT and electrical equipment can you collect?

Collection enquiries can include laptops, desktop computers, workstations, servers, monitors, printers, scanners, networking equipment, docking stations, mobile phones, tablets, UPS equipment and other office electronics.

Can you destroy SSDs and hard drives?

HDD and SSD destruction can be requested. Certificates of destruction may also be available where agreed as part of the service scope.

What happens to equipment after collection?

Equipment is assessed for reuse, refurbishment or component recovery where practical. Items unsuitable for reuse are directed towards responsible recycling arrangements.

Do you provide regular business collections?

Yes. Businesses and organisations can request one-off, weekly, monthly or quarterly collection arrangements, subject to suitability and availability.

Which areas do you cover?

Initial enquiries are welcomed from Manchester, Salford, Trafford, Stockport, Bolton, Bury, Oldham, Rochdale, Warrington and Cheshire. Larger collections outside these areas may also be considered.
